This module records on manufacturing orders the quantities read or
weighed by the user, using the engine of ``stock_capture``. It has no
screen of its own.

- **Product to manufacture:** a captured quantity adds to the quantity
  to produce and its lot becomes the lot to produce, as Odoo's barcode
  application does. The finished move is not picked, because Odoo would
  take it as already produced. A quantity captured into a destination
  package gets its own finished line in that package, and changing the
  quantity to produce keeps those lines.
- **Components:** a captured quantity is taken from the reservation of
  the component, keeping the rest reserved. Odoo then keeps the captured
  quantity when the quantity to produce changes, and components not
  captured are consumed as the bill of materials says. A component not
  in the order gets a new move.
- **Byproducts:** only the captured quantity is produced for a byproduct
  with captured quantities.

When the order is marked as done, the lines left unpicked on the moves
with captured quantities are dropped before the consumption warning, so
the warning compares what was captured with the bill of materials.

Usage
=====

Other modules capture quantities through:

- ``mrp.production._capture_product(product, quantity, uom=None, lot=None, location=None, package=None, owner=None, result_package=None)``,
  which sends the product to the methods below
- ``mrp.production._capture_finished(quantity, uom=None, lot=None, result_package=None)``
  and
  ``mrp.production._capture_undo_finished(quantity, uom=None, result_package=None)``
  for the product to manufacture
- ``mrp.production._capture_component(product, quantity, uom=None, **characteristics)``
  for components
- ``stock.move.line._capture_undo(quantity=None)`` of ``stock_capture``
  for components and byproducts
